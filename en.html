Our Brands

Curated with an adventurous soul

Each brand in our collection is handpicked — for its authenticity, its story, and its respect for nature. Brands that don't just dress you: they equip you for the journey.

California, USA

ROARK

ROARK is more than clothing — it's a mindset. Born from the road, the dust of distant trails, and the first light on the water, the brand lives by one rule: adventure never fails. Each collection is shaped by a real journey — from the archipelago of the Philippines to the volcanic landscapes of Iceland — and every piece is built to handle salt, wind, and the unknown. For those who don't just chase adventure — they wear it.

Hawaii, USA

OLUKAI

Born in Hawaii and guided by the spirit of aloha, OluKai creates premium footwear that blends comfort, durability, and timeless design. Each pair is made with respect for nature and a deep commitment to sustainability. Wearing OluKai means carrying the generosity of the islands, the strength of the ocean, and the certainty that every step honors the land beneath you.

California, USA

MELIN

Born under the eternal sun of Southern California, melin isn't just a brand — it's a revolution in the world of accessories. It all started with the bold vision of two close friends, Brian McDonell and Corey Roth. Driven by an unbreakable passion for outdoor living and urban culture, they refused to accept the ordinary. Their answer? Redefine the rules of the game. With obsessive attention to detail and luxury materials never seen before, melin took the classic casual hat and elevated it to the status of a premium work of art. More than just sun protection, a melin is a statement of style, performance, and sophistication for those who demand the best in every detail of life.

Surfside, California, USA

KATIN

For over 70 years, Walter and Nancy Katin cut boat sails to create the world's first surf shorts. What started as a request from a young surfer named Corky Carroll — who would later become one of the first professionals in history — is now a surf culture icon. Katin is living proof that the most authentic things are born from necessity, ingenuity, and love for the sea.

Visit brand website →
Norway

DB JOURNEY

Founded in 2009 by legendary freeskier Jon Olsson and engineer Truls Brataas, Db was born to solve a simple problem: how to carry what really matters. From backpacks to travel bags, each product is designed with Nordic precision, snow-ready durability, and Scandinavian elegance. For those who travel far, in style, with everything they need.

Sweden

SUNTRIBE

Suntribe believes that protecting your skin shouldn't cost the planet's health. Their sunscreens are 100% free from synthetic chemicals, using only natural and organic ingredients that respect both your body and the oceans. From the warm sands of the Algarve to the freezing waves of Ericeira, Suntribe protects you so you can focus on what really matters: living in the moment.

About Us

Humans & Nature

Our acumen, in tune with nature, allows us to choose materials, partners, and solutions that respect the natural balance without compromising performance or durability.

We are a team with over 20 years of experience in the action sports universe. Wilderness Brothers was born from the desire to represent alternative and emerging brands that make a difference — brands that make our lives easier and more inspiring.

Whether in the city, at a meeting or a party, whether looking for the next wave or in the most remote mountains where gear is indispensable: your comfort defines your confidence. And your confidence defines how you see the world.

We believe in a fairer market and a more sustainable industry. Buy less, but better. With knowledge and the right advice. Offering quality is our way of protecting the future — the Planet, the People, and the businesses that walk in harmony with us.
